How to read this

If 100 people now live in the Pittsburgh MSA and moved in the last year, about 70 already lived in Allegheny, Armstrong, Beaver, Butler, Fayette, Lawrence, Washington, or Westmoreland County. Most of those 70 came from the suburbs and outlying counties, not from the City of Pittsburgh.

This is not return migration. The ACS only asks where you lived one year ago. Someone who grew up here, spent a decade elsewhere, and moved back last year is counted as arriving from outside.
