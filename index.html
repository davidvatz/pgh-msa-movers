    How to read this

    This is not return migration. The ACS only asks where you lived one year ago. Someone who grew up here, spent a decade elsewhere, and moved back last year is counted as arriving from outside.

    U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2024 1-year estimates, table B07201. Puerto Rico metros are omitted; that table covers the 50 states and D.C. Margin of error is the ACS 90% confidence interval.
